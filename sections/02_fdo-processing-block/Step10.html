[S-10]: FDO Data Access

This step attempts to return the FDO’s data bit-sequence or its location. It assumes that was completed and that its status indicates that a 0.FDO/Data field is present.
Inputs
This step expects that had a Mandatory_Attributes_Status of COMPLETE, DATA_ONLY, TYPE_MISSING, or PROFILE_MISSING. This step expects the output of : exactly one well-structured FDO record.
Outputs
Depending on the content of the 0.FDO/Data field, the output is one of the following:
  • a data bit-sequence
  • a location where the data bit-sequence is stored
  • the string self
  • a null value
Expected behavior
The Client inspects each of the Validated_Profiles and finds their respective 0.FDO/Data attributes to find the data attributes. The client SHOULD make the best effort to provide the means to access the data depending on the manner in which the FDO references its data.
